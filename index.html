Not your old vending machine

Micro market vs. vending machine.

A micro market is an open, grab-and-go store: real shelves, a glass-front cooler, fresh food, and tap-to-pay self-checkout. A vending machine is a locked box with a short list of packaged snacks. Same space, a completely different experience.

Micro market: grab & go

  • Open shelves and a cooler you walk right up to
  • Fresh meals, drinks, snacks, coffee, and essentials
  • Handle products, read labels, grab several at once
  • Fast tap-to-pay self-checkout
  • A real amenity to show on a tour

Traditional vending

  • A locked box, no touching before you buy
  • A short list of packaged, shelf-stable items
  • One item at a time; jams and dead rows
  • Coin or card slot
  • Limited selection

FAQ

Straight answers for property managers.

Don't see your question? Call (385) 374-0315 or email hello@cachemarkets.com and we'll answer it directly.

How much does a micro market cost the property?

Nothing. Cache Markets pays for the equipment, install, product, stocking, and service. Residents and employees pay for what they buy at the kiosk.

What does the property need to provide?

A visible spot people pass every day, a standard outlet, and Wi-Fi for the kiosk. That's it.

How much space does it take?

A Cache market can fit in a footprint as small as 30 x 30 inches. Common spots are a clubhouse corner, a lobby wall, a mail or package room, or an amenity floor.

Are you insured?

Yes. Cache Markets is licensed & insured, and we provide a certificate of insurance (COI) for your location before install.

How long does install take?

Install timing is agreed with each property in the letter of intent, and we confirm the date with you before anything is installed.

What if something goes missing?

The equipment and inventory belong to Cache Markets. Your office team does not handle cash, inventory, or refunds.

Can we cancel?

Yes. The notice period is agreed per property in the letter of intent, and Cache removes the equipment.

Who signs off on it?

Usually the community manager and their regional manager or the owner. We are happy to bring the mockup and LOI to whoever makes the call.
